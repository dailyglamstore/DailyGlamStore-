💎 About Us
Welcome to Daily Glam Store — a curated fashion accessories platform for style lovers who enjoy trendy and value-for-money finds. We showcase a carefully curated selection of jewellery, handbags, wallets and everyday glam essentials for modern fashion lovers. Daily Glam Store operates in collaboration with Deepak Cosmetic Store – Main Bazar, Gurdaspur, a trusted local retail business serving customers with reliability and consistency. For product enquiries or order assistance, customers can easily connect with our team on WhatsApp for quick support.
🛍️ How Ordering Works?
Step 1:
Swipe right to check all product pictures. Once you stop at the product picture you like, click on the “Order on WhatsApp” button.
Step 2:
An enquiry message will be automatically generated with the exact image you selected, product details and a preview link so you can cross-check the colour chosen afterwards.
Step 3:
Our sales team checks product availability and confirms your preferred delivery option:
  • Either Home delivery to your address
  • Or Local pickup from Deepak Cosmetic Store, Main Bazar, Gurdaspur
Step 4:
Once you confirm your delivery preference, our team shares the secure scan code for payment.
Step 5:
After successful payment, your order is processed and securely packed for dispatch.

Note: Final delivery is completed through your preferred delivery option within the promised timeframe.
