For oily skin, pick gel or foaming cleansers containing salicylic acid or niacinamide. Dry skin benefits from hydrating, cream-based cleansers with hyaluronic acid or glycerin. Sensitive skin requires gentle, fragrance-free formulas.

Vitamin C serum is best applied in the morning after cleansing and before moisturizing and sunscreen. It helps protect skin from environmental damage, reduces dark spots, and boosts overall glow.

💇 Haircare FAQs

Oiling 1 to 2 times a week before washing is usually sufficient. Avoid leaving oil overnight if you have an oily scalp or are prone to scalp acne, as it can clog hair follicles.

Apply hair serum to damp or towel-dried hair, focusing on the mid-lengths to ends. Avoid applying serum directly to the roots or scalp to prevent greasy buildup.

Conditioner can be used every time you wash your hair. If you wash daily, choose a lightweight formula and apply only to hair lengths, keeping it away from your scalp.
