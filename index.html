💎 About Us

🛍️ How Ordering Works
Step 1: Stop at the picture you like, then click on the “Order on WhatsApp” button.

Step 2: An enquiry message will be automatically generated with the exact image you selected, product details and a preview link so you can cross-check the colour chosen afterwards.

Step 3: Our sales team will check product availability, confirm your shipping address, and then share the scan code for payment.

Step 4: Once payment is confirmed, your order is finalized and will reach your doorstep within the prescribed delivery time mentioned on the website.

🏬 For Local Customers: You may also choose to have your order delivered to our local partner store for convenient pickup.
