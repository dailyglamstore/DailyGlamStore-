Skincare Buying Guides

Explore simple skincare buying guides for face wash, serum, sunscreen, and moisturiser based on different skin types and concerns.

🧼 Face Wash Buying Guide

Explore face wash options based on skin type like oily, dry, and sensitive, along with popular ingredients like vitamin C, salicylic acid, and aloe vera.

🍊Vitamin C face wash

  • Supports a brighter, fresher look.
  • Useful for dull-looking skin goals.

💧Hyaluronic acid face wash

  • Helps cleanse without a stripped feel.
  • Good for normal, dry, or dehydrated skin.

🧪Salicylic acid face wash

  • Helps clear excess oil and pore buildup.
  • Best for oily, acne-prone, clog-prone skin.

🌿Aloe vera face wash

  • Known for a gentle, soothing cleanse.
  • Often suits sensitive or easily-irritated skin.

🌾Rice water face wash

  • Usually a mild, soft-feel cleansing option.
  • Can suit everyday use for a smooth finish.
Texture Tip :
  • For oily or acne-prone skin, gel based face wash works best as it helps control excess oil.
  • For dry or sensitive skin, cream based face wash feels gentler and maintains hydration.
Buying Tip :
  • Choose a face wash based on your skin type first, then pick one ingredient that targets your main concern.
Do’s :
  • Wash face twice daily with a gentle cleanser
  • Use lukewarm water for better results
Don’t’s :
  • Don’t over-wash as it can strip natural oils
  • Don’t switch products too frequently

💧 Face Serum Buying Guide

Explore face serum options for concerns like dull skin, dehydration, acne, and uneven tone using ingredients like vitamin C, hyaluronic acid, and niacinamide.

🍊Vitamin C serum

  • Provides antioxidant and brightening support.
  • Best for dull-looking or uneven-tone concerns.

💧Hyaluronic acid serum

  • Boosts hydration and a plumper skin feel.
  • Great when skin feels dehydrated or tight.

✨Niacinamide serum

  • Supports brightness and more even-looking tone.
  • Often acne-prone and combination-skin friendly.
Texture Tip :
  • Water based serums absorb quickly and are ideal for layering.
  • Oil based serums are better for dry skin and night-time repair.
Buying Tip :
  • Focus on one concern at a time instead of using multiple serums together.
Do’s :
  • Apply on clean, slightly damp skin
  • Use consistently for visible results
Don’t’s :
  • Don’t mix too many active ingredients
  • Don’t apply excessive quantity

☀️ Sunscreen Buying Guide

Explore sunscreen options based on SPF levels like SPF 30, 40, and 50, along with gel and cream formulas suitable for different skin types.

☀️SPF 30 sunscreen

  • Good for regular daily use with limited sun exposure.
  • Suitable for office, home, or short outdoor time.

🌤SPF 40 sunscreen

  • Balanced option for regular outdoor movement.
  • Good for daily use when sun exposure is moderate.

🔆SPF 50 sunscreen

  • Best for strong sun, travel, or long outdoor time.
  • Suitable for high exposure and extra protection needs.

💧Gel sunscreen

  • Feels lighter and less greasy on skin.
  • Best for oily or acne-prone skin types.

🧴Cream sunscreen

  • Gives more comfort and moisturising feel.
  • Better for dry or normal skin types.
Texture Tip :
  • For oily or acne-prone skin, gel sunscreen usually feels lighter and less greasy.
  • For dry or normal skin, cream sunscreen feels more comfortable and moisturising.
Buying Tip :
  • Choose SPF based on your exposure level — SPF 30 for mostly indoor daily use, SPF 40 for regular outdoor movement, and SPF 50 for stronger sun exposure.
Do’s :
  • Apply 15–20 minutes before sun exposure
  • Reapply every 2–3 hours outdoors
Don’t’s :
  • Don’t skip sunscreen on cloudy days
  • Don’t apply too little product

🧴 Moisturiser Buying Guide

Explore moisturisers based on hydration, oil control, barrier repair, and glow, suitable for dry, oily, and combination skin types.

💧Hydra Glow moisturiser

  • Deep hydration with a natural glow finish.
  • Best for dull, dehydrated, or tired-looking skin.

🍊Vitamin C brightening moisturiser

  • Helps improve skin brightness and even tone.
  • Good for pigmentation or uneven skin tone.

🌿Oil-free moisturiser

  • Lightweight hydration without clogging pores.
  • Ideal for oily or acne-prone skin.

🛡Barrier repair moisturiser

  • Strengthens skin barrier and locks in moisture.
  • Best for dry, sensitive, or damaged skin.
Texture Tip :
  • For everyday use, choose a lightweight moisturiser that absorbs quickly without feeling sticky.
  • Richer formulas are better when skin feels dry, rough, or exposed to harsh weather.
Buying Tip :
  • Select your moisturiser based on your skin concern — hydration, brightness, oil control, or barrier repair — instead of only texture.
Do’s :
  • Apply moisturiser on slightly damp skin for better absorption
  • Use lighter moisturisers during day and richer ones at night
Don’t’s :
  • Don’t ignore your skin concern while choosing moisturiser
  • Don’t use the same moisturiser in all seasons

Haircare Buying Guides

Explore haircare buying guides for shampoo, conditioner, serum, and oil based on scalp type, hair concerns, and styling needs.

🧴 Shampoo Buying Guide

Explore shampoo options based on scalp type like oily, dry, and dandruff-prone, including sulphate-free, keratin, and anti-dandruff formulas.

🧪Sulphate-Free Shampoo

  • Gentle cleansing without harsh chemicals.
  • Suitable for dry, frizzy, or treated hair.

✨Keratin Shampoo

  • Helps smooth hair and reduce rough texture.
  • Best for frizzy or damaged hair.

🧅Onion Shampoo

  • Popular for hair fall control routines.
  • Suitable for weak or thinning hair.

🌿Biotin / Growth Support Shampoo

  • Supports stronger and fuller-looking hair.
  • Best for weak or thinning hair concerns.

❄️Anti-Dandruff / Scalp Care Shampoo

  • Helps control flakes and scalp irritation.
  • Suitable for oily or dandruff-prone scalp.
Texture Tip :
  • Gel-based shampoos are lighter and suit oily scalp.
  • Cream-based shampoos are gentler and better for dry or damaged hair.
Buying Tip :
  • Choose shampoo based on scalp type first, then target your main concern like dandruff, hair fall, or dryness.
Do’s :
  • Wash 2–3 times a week based on scalp oiliness
  • Dilute shampoo slightly with water before applying
Don’t’s :
  • Don’t apply shampoo directly on hair lengths
  • Don’t over-wash as it can dry out the scalp

💧 Conditioner Buying Guide

Explore conditioner options for frizz control, repair, and hydration based on hair type like dry, damaged, or chemically treated hair.

✨Keratin Conditioner

  • Helps smooth hair and reduce frizz.
  • Best for rough or damaged hair.

🛠Deep Repair Conditioner

  • Supports hair weakened by heat or styling.
  • Suitable for damaged or chemically treated hair.

💧Hydrating Conditioner

  • Adds moisture and softness to dry hair.
  • Ideal for rough or dull hair.

🌿Sulphate-Free Conditioner

  • Gentle conditioning without heavy chemicals.
  • Suitable for sensitive or treated hair.

🌬Frizz Control Conditioner

  • Improves smoothness and manageability.
  • Best for humid conditions and flyaways.
Texture Tip :
  • Lightweight conditioners suit fine or oily hair.
  • Richer conditioners are better for thick or dry hair.
Buying Tip :
  • Pick conditioner based on hair damage level and texture, not just fragrance or brand.
Do’s :
  • Apply only on mid-lengths and ends
  • Leave for 2–3 minutes before rinsing
Don’t’s :
  • Don’t apply conditioner on scalp
  • Don’t skip conditioner after shampoo

✨ Hair Serum Buying Guide

Explore hair serums for frizz control, shine, heat protection, and daily styling based on different hair needs and textures.

✨Frizz control serum

  • Smoothens flyaways and rough texture.
  • Ideal for frizzy hair.

💧Dry hair serum

  • Adds shine and softness instantly.
  • Best for dull or dry hair.

🔥Heat protection serum

  • Protects hair from heat styling tools.
  • Use before straightening or blow drying.

🌟Shine enhancing serum

  • Gives a glossy and polished look.
  • Perfect for styling and finishing.

🌿Lightweight daily serum

  • Keeps hair manageable throughout the day.
  • Suitable for regular use.
Texture Tip :
  • Silicone-based serums smooth frizz instantly.
  • Oil-based serums give nourishment and shine.
Buying Tip :
  • Choose serum based on your purpose, such as frizz control, heat protection, or shine.
Do’s :
  • Use a small amount on damp or dry hair
  • Focus on lengths and ends
Don’t’s :
  • Don’t overuse serum as it can make hair greasy
  • Don’t apply serum on the scalp

🌿 Hair Oil Buying Guide

Explore hair oils for hair fall control, scalp nourishment, and dryness using options like onion oil, coconut oil, and herbal blends.

🧅Onion Oil

  • Helps with hair fall control.
  • Supports stronger looking roots.

🌿Black Seed Oil

  • Supports scalp health.
  • Useful for dry, stressed scalp.

🥥Coconut Oil

  • Deep nourishment for dry lengths.
  • Helps reduce roughness and frizz.

🌰Almond Oil

  • Supports smoothness and shine.
  • Helps hair feel soft and manageable.

🍃Herbal Oil

  • Supports overall strengthening.
  • Great for regular weekly oiling.
Texture Tip :
  • Light oils like almond or onion suit regular use.
  • Heavy oils like coconut-based blends are better for deep nourishment.
Buying Tip :
  • Choose oil based on your goal, such as hair fall control, dryness, or scalp care.
Do’s :
  • Massage gently into the scalp
  • Leave for 1–2 hours before washing
Don’t’s :
  • Don’t leave oil too long if your scalp is very oily
  • Don’t apply excessive quantity
